# Bilingual teaching track

This repository is **bilingual for teaching**:

| Layer | Language | Role |
|-------|----------|------|
| `docs/zh/` | Chinese | Concepts, intuition, classroom-style walkthrough |
| Root README (top) | Chinese + English | Front door |
| `pipeline/`, tool pages, EVALUATION gates, METHODS templates | **English** | Runnable / citable / journal-facing |

Chinese pages link to English ops docs; they do not replace them. Teaching includes dedicated WHY lessons (`00_*` and later WHY units) plus short `## 为什么` blocks in major lessons.  
Do not put private paths or Chinese-only METHODS into a paper supplement without an English twin.

Structure sibling: [gene-structure-annotation `docs/zh/`](https://github.com/Xuzhen-Li/gene-structure-annotation/tree/main/docs/zh).

Full zh curriculum listed in [`docs/zh/README.md`](zh/README.md).
